Here are two independently authored PE32 DLL reproducers using the normal
whole-binary commands:

https://github.com/humdrum00001010/revng/tree/61e6d575cc4c93bf7b2b0a6d39009dc0e814d235/reproducers/issues/649

The fixtures contain named, ordinal-only, aliased, forwarded, data, and hole
export entries. The original export tables have three unique executable
targets, while both official quick and project pipelines produce a model with
zero functions and an includes-only `functions.c`. Exact build/run commands,
binary truth inventories, models, archives, hashes, and logs are included.
